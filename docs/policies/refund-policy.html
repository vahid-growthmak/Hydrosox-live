Your right to cancel

If you are a consumer in the UK you have the right to cancel your order for any reason within 14 days of receiving it, without giving a reason. This is your statutory right under the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013, and nothing in this policy reduces it.

To cancel, tell us before the 14 days are up. Email {{ email }} or call {{ phone }}. You do not have to use a particular form of words — a clear statement that you are cancelling is enough.

Once you have told us you are cancelling, you have a further 14 days to send the goods back.

Returning the goods

Socks are a hygiene-sensitive product. We ask that you return them unworn and in their original packaging, with any seal intact. You are allowed to handle and inspect them as you would in a shop; if they have been worn beyond that we may reduce your refund to reflect the loss in value.

[CONFIRM] Who pays return postage, and the return address, are set out here once decided. Under the regulations we may require you to bear the cost of return provided we tell you so before you order.

Refunds

We will refund the price you paid, plus the standard outbound delivery charge if you paid one, within 14 days of receiving the goods back or of receiving proof that you have sent them. Refunds go to the original payment method.

If you chose a delivery service more expensive than our standard option, we refund the standard cost rather than the premium.

Faulty or misdescribed goods

Your rights here are separate from cancellation and are not time-limited to 14 days. Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 goods must be of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose and as described. If they are not:

  • within 30 days of delivery you can reject them and ask for a full refund;
  • after 30 days you can ask for a repair or replacement, and a refund or price reduction if that does not resolve it.

Contact {{ email }} or {{ phone }} and tell us what has gone wrong. We do not charge for returning faulty goods.

What is not covered

Wear that comes from normal use, damage from abrasion, damage from washing against the care instructions, and damage from alteration are not faults. The care instructions are published on the site.
